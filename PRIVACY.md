# PaperFeed Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

PaperFeed ("the app") is developed by [YOUR NAME] ("I"). This policy explains what happens to your data.

## What the app stores

Your sources, lists, ratings, notes, saved articles and settings are stored **only on your device**. I do not run any server, and the app contains no analytics, tracking or advertising code.

## Network requests

To show articles, the app contacts public scholarly services directly from your device:
- Crossref, OpenAlex, arXiv and Semantic Scholar
- doi.org
- the journals' publishers (APS, Elsevier, IOP, Taylor & Francis, Annual Reviews)
- any feeds you add yourself

These requests contain only what is needed: journal identifiers, search terms, DOIs and article titles. If you choose to enter a contact email in Settings, it is sent to Crossref and OpenAlex as their "polite pool" identifier. If you enter an Elsevier API key, it is sent only to api.elsevier.com. Each service's own privacy policy applies to the requests it receives.

## Google Drive backup (optional)

If you turn on Google Drive backup, the app asks Google for permission to access **only files it creates** (the `drive.file` scope). It writes one file, `PaperFeed-backup.json`, to **your** Google Drive. I never receive this file or your Google credentials. You can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

## Purchases (optional)

Tips are processed entirely by Google Play. I receive no payment details.

## Notifications

Notifications are generated on your device from the sources you follow.
